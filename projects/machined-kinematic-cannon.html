Machined Kinematic Cannon

WhereUC San Diego machining seminar
When2025
SkillsManual and CNC milling and turning, GD&T, DFM, tolerance stacking
OutcomeEvery component machined to ±0.003″

I machined and assembled this kinematic ball bearing launcher for a machining seminar I took at UC San Diego. The drawings and the Fusion 360 assembly were pre-designed; I used them to learn the fundamentals of manual and CNC milling and turning, along with a first working exposure to GD&T, DFM and tolerance stacking.

The cannon is made from manually milled aluminum base plates and struts, a manually lathed aluminum firing pin, a CNC-milled Delrin chassis and ball bearing tray, and a CNC-lathed nozzle. Every component was machined to ±0.003″.
