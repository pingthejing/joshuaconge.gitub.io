Point-of-Care Parasympathetic Monitor

A portable device that combines a heart rate monitor, spirometer and blood pressure cuff to measure heart rate variability.

WhereSenior Design Capstone, UC San Diego
When2026
SkillsC++, Arduino, AD8232, Pan-Tompkins peak detection, ECG signal processing
OutcomeSampled ECG signals at 250 Hz and extracted cardiac parameters at around 95% accuracy (heart rate within 3.3% of a reference monitor), contributing to our team winning Best Overall Project at the UCSD Senior Capstone Poster Presentation.

This was my MAE 156B senior design project, sponsored by Dr. Stephen Bickler of UC San Diego Health and Rady Children’s Hospital. The device is a compact, portable monitor that measures heart rate variability during deep breathing and a 5-minute blood pressure cuff hold, plus heart rate, breath volume, and blood pressure. It was designed for use in Sub-Saharan Africa, specifically Mozambique.

  • Built the ECG acquisition system around an AD8232 sensor and an Arduino.
  • Wrote Pan-Tompkins peak detection and RR-interval analysis in C++ to calculate heart rate and RMSSD, handling signal artifacts and lead-off conditions.
