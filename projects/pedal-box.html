Baja SAE Pedal Box

A lighter pedal box for off-road Baja SAE competition.

WhereTriton Baja, UC San Diego
When2024
SkillsSolidWorks, FEA, MATLAB, TIG welding, laser cutting
OutcomePedal box came out 12% lighter, then welded and tested.

A mobile, sustainable pedal box for off-road Baja SAE competition. Modeled and drawn in SolidWorks to ANSI standard, it came out 12% lighter.

  • Fabricated the pedal box and the brake and accelerator pedals with TIG welding and laser cutting.
  • Analyzed the pedal box and chassis under several load cases with SolidWorks FEA and MATLAB, checking deflection, yield strength, and material deformation.
