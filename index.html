Hey, I’m Joshua.

I design parts, analyze them, and build them. I finished my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at UC San Diego in June 2026 and I’m staying on for a master’s in Applied Mechanics, with a minor in Mathematics.

I work mostly in SolidWorks, Onshape, and Ansys, with GD&T and Design for Manufacturing guiding how I draw things. I also fabricate what I design with TIG welding, laser cutting, CNC, and composite layups, and I write MATLAB, C++, and Python when a project needs code.

So far that includes an inspection fixture at General Atomics, the airframe of an electric wildfire-response hexacopter with Team Mirage, a medical monitoring device for senior design, and a lightweight pedal box for Triton Baja SAE.
