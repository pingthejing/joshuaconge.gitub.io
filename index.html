Hey, I’m Joshua.

I’m a mechanical engineer who finished my B.S. at UC San Diego in June 2026 and is staying on for a master’s in Applied Mechanics, with a minor in Mathematics. I’m mostly focused on mechanical design and analysis (SolidWorks, Onshape, Ansys, GD&T, DFM), and I build what I design with TIG welding, laser cutting, CNC machining, and composite layup. I also write code when a project calls for it (MATLAB, C++, Python).

Some of the things I’ve worked on so far include an inspection fixture at General Atomics Electromagnetics, the airframe of an electric wildfire-response hexacopter with UCSD’s Vertical Flight Society (Team Mirage), a portable medical monitoring device for my MAE senior design project, and a lightweight pedal box for Triton Baja SAE.

A few of these projects I’ve highlighted below. You can see the rest on my projects page, and my résumé is here as well.
