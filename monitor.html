  • Point-of-Care Parasympathetic Monitor

    UC San Diego MAE 156B senior design. Sponsor: Dr. Stephen Bickler, UC San Diego Health. C++, Arduino, AD8232.

    A compact, portable monitor that measures heart rate variability during deep breathing and a 5-minute blood pressure cuff hold, plus heart rate, breath volume, and blood pressure. It was designed for use in Sub-Saharan Africa, specifically Mozambique. Team: Joshua Conge, Marshall Nakada, Natan Benveniste, Seongeun Yoo.

    • Built the ECG acquisition system around an AD8232 sensor and an Arduino.
    • Wrote Pan-Tompkins peak detection and RR-interval analysis in C++ to calculate heart rate and RMSSD, handling signal artifacts and lead-off conditions.
